Who am I?

With more than two years of experience in game development and a foundation in software development tools, I'm a passionate Game Producer who loves supporting and guiding others. Currently enrolled at Breda University of Applied Sciences in the Netherlands, I thrive in and enjoy team-oriented settings, excelling in clear communication and collaboration.

In my producer role, I concentrate on keeping projects on schedule, creating seamless workflows, and supporting my team's creative vision. I love collaborating with various disciplines, tackling challenges, and ensuring everyone has the necessary resources to work and excel.

Outside my studies, I am a student ambassador and help manage the BUas Games Instagram account. I connect with fellow students and showcase the impressive work at BUas. I continually look for opportunities for growth, improvement, and innovative approaches to enhance the efficiency and enjoyment of game development.
