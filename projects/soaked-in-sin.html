Producer work

On Soaked in Sin

01Scope Management

To keep our project on track, I created a detailed scope breakdown that allowed the team to stay focused and efficient throughout development. After the first 12 hours, we had a clear vision for our game, which we refined through discussions with the entire team. Using that vision, I built a comprehensive scope breakdown in about an hour, ensuring everyone understood the project's direction and priorities.

The breakdown was based on a modified version of my own template, which I developed as part of my game production toolkit. This structured approach gave us a clear overview of remaining tasks, helped us allocate resources effectively, and provided insight into our progress. Being able to track our momentum boosted team morale and confidence, keeping everyone motivated as we worked toward completing the game within the tight deadline.

02Communication

As the project's central communication hub, I ensured that everyone knew who was working on what and could quickly connect with the right person when they needed assistance. I kept track of tasks using Miro, providing a clear overview of the team's progress and workload. While this approach worked well, I'm exploring ways to develop a more streamlined tool for even better team coordination in future projects.

I also led all group meetings where key project decisions were made. With only 48 hours to complete the game, time efficiency was crucial, so I kept meetings focused and concise, allowing the team to spend as much time as possible on development. By maintaining clear and structured communication, I helped the team stay aligned, solve problems quickly, and keep momentum throughout the jam.
